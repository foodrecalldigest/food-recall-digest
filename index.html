HIGH RISK  ·  FDA  ·  Sep 23
Berlin Seeds Alfalfa Sprouting Seed
⚠️
PROBLEM
E. coli (STEC) and/or Salmonella
🔍
LOOK FOR
All sizes: ¼ lb, ½ lb, 1 lb, 5 lb, 50 lb
 
5 and 50 lb bags: lot SAL2‑24JB (bottom right of label)
 
Bought Feb 13 – Aug 23, 2026
 
🩺
ILLNESSES
Linked by FDA to a sprout outbreak: 55 sick in 15 states, 4 hospitalized (as of Sep 24).
📍
WHERE SOLD
✓ CONFIRMED
Berlin Seeds store in Millersburg, OH, and the Berlin Seeds website (shipped to 45 states + Puerto Rico).
➜  Stop using. Don’t sprout, plant, give away, or eat sprouts grown from it. Seal in a bag, throw away, and sanitize containers.
