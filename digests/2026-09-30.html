●  Germs & toxinsFDA · Announced Sep 28, posted Sep 29

E. coli. 13 sick, 3 with kidney failure.

Do this: Do not eat.

Sierra Nevada Graziers Raw Milk Cheeses

What’s wrong

  • E. coli O26:H11 (STEC)
  • 13 sick in 9 states (CA, CO, GA, KY, MI, NV, OR, TN, UT). 8 hospitalized, 3 with kidney failure (HUS). Over half are children 5 and under.

Check your kitchen

Retail packs (5)

  • Monterey Jack 8 oz, UPC 6‑87652‑16400‑9
  • Jalapeño Jack 8 oz, UPC 6‑87652‑16403‑0
  • Medium Cheddar 8 oz, UPC 6‑87652‑16506‑8
  • Sharp Cheddar 8 oz, UPC 6‑87652‑16507‑5
  • Medium Cheddar 16 oz, UPC 6‑87652‑16501‑3

Food service (2)

  • 5 lb loaves, items 6425, 6426, 6510, 6511
  • 40 lb cheddar blocks, items 6515, 6516

Where it was sold

Stores not named in the notice
  • Retailers nationwide and online (FDA/CDC).
Possibly sold here (not confirmed for the recalled lots): Company store in Willows, CA; Farm Fresh To You; Azure Standard
Do this:
  • Do not eat.
  • Return for a refund or throw away, then clean surfaces it touched.

Read the official notice: FDA notice · CDC outbreak

●  Germs & toxinsFDA · Sep 23

E. coli / Salmonella. Linked to 55 sick.

Do this: Stop using.
Show details

Berlin Seeds Alfalfa Sprouting Seed

What’s wrong

  • E. coli (STEC) and/or Salmonella
  • Linked by FDA to a sprout outbreak: 55 sick in 15 states, 4 hospitalized (as of Sep 24).

Check your kitchen

  • All sizes: ¼ lb, ½ lb, 1 lb, 5 lb, 50 lb
  • 5 and 50 lb bags: lot SAL2‑24JB (bottom right of label)
  • Bought Feb 13 to Aug 23, 2026

Where it was sold

✓ Confirmed by the official notice
  • Berlin Seeds store in Millersburg, OH, and the Berlin Seeds website (shipped to 45 states + Puerto Rico).
Do this:
  • Stop using.
  • Don’t sprout, plant, give away, or eat sprouts grown from it.
  • Seal in a bag, throw away, and sanitize containers.

Read the official notice: FDA notice · Outbreak page
