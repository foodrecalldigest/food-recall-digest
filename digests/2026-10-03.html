▸Recall details
 
▸ALLERGEN  ·  FDA  ·  Oct 1 (FDA posted Oct 3)  ·  Saint Francis frozen pizzas, Cincinnati area
Saint Francis Frozen Pizzas (Pepperoni, Plain Cheese, Supreme, Mushroom & Onion)
⚠️
PROBLEM
Undeclared soy. The label does not list the soy sub-ingredients soybean oil and soy lecithin. People allergic to soy could have a serious or life-threatening reaction.
🔍
LOOK FOR
Saint Francis frozen pizza, Pepperoni
 
Saint Francis frozen pizza, Plain Cheese
 
Saint Francis frozen pizza, Supreme
 
Saint Francis frozen pizza, Mushroom and Onion
 
Sizes, UPCs, lot codes and dates: not stated in the notice (all products are covered)
 
🩺
ILLNESSES
No illnesses reported.
📍
WHERE SOLD
✓ CONFIRMED
Sold by Saint Francis Apizza LLC at 11 shops in Ohio and Kentucky (Cincinnati, Milford, Carlisle, Dayton, Fort Thomas, Covington) and online at cincyfavorites.com.

➜  If you are allergic to soy, don’t eat it. Throw it away or return it to Saint Francis Apizza LLC for a full refund. Questions: Alex Plattner, 971‑221‑7086.
