Saint Francis frozen pizzas have soy that isn't on the label.

Four Saint Francis frozen pizza varieties sold at 11 Cincinnati-area shops and cincyfavorites.com don’t list soy on the label. No other new recalls; outbreak counts unchanged.

Show details

Saint Francis Frozen Pizzas (Pepperoni, Plain Cheese, Supreme, Mushroom & Onion)

🍕

What’s wrong

  • Undeclared soy.
  • The label does not list the soy sub-ingredients soybean oil and soy lecithin.
  • People allergic to soy could have a serious or life-threatening reaction.
  • No illnesses reported.

Check your kitchen

  • Saint Francis frozen pizza, Pepperoni
  • Saint Francis frozen pizza, Plain Cheese
  • Saint Francis frozen pizza, Supreme
  • Saint Francis frozen pizza, Mushroom and Onion
  • Sizes, UPCs, lot codes and dates: not stated in the notice (all products are covered)

Where it was sold

✓ Confirmed by the official notice
  • Sold by Saint Francis Apizza LLC at 11 shops in Ohio and Kentucky (Cincinnati, Milford, Carlisle, Dayton, Fort Thomas, Covington) and online at cincyfavorites.com.

Every store and address is listed below

Do this:
  • If you are allergic to soy, don’t eat it.
  • Throw it away or return it to Saint Francis Apizza LLC for a full refund.
  • Questions: Alex Plattner, 971‑221‑7086.
