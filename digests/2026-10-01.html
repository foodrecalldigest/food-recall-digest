●  Germs & toxinsFDA · Oct 1

Listeria. Walmart stores nationwide.

Do this: Don’t eat it.
Show details

bettergoods Authentic Italian Lemon Alfredo Fettuccine

What’s wrong

  • Possible Listeria monocytogenes, found by Washington and Florida state agriculture sampling.
  • Listeria is most dangerous for pregnant women, young children, older adults and people with weak immune systems.
  • None reported to date.

Check your kitchen

  • Frozen, 22 oz yellow plastic package
  • UPC 194346442706
  • ALL lot codes are now recalled (the Sep 15 recall covered only lots L6079C and L6080C, expiration 09/19/2027 and 09/20/2027)

Where it was sold

✓ Confirmed by the official notice
  • Walmart stores nationwide (FDA notice).
Do this:
  • Don’t eat it.
  • Return it to the store for a full refund.
  • Questions: sales@giasfoods.com.

Read the official notice: FDA notice (Oct 1, all lots) · FDA notice (Sep 15, 2 lots)
