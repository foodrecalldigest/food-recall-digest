Power Plate Meals meatloaf

Still open. We check every evening and update this page if USDA closes it.

◆  AllergenUSDA · Jun 18

Undeclared soy. MN, ND, SD.

Do this: If you’re allergic to soy, don’t eat it.
Show details

Power Plate Meals Meatloaf with Garlic Mashed Potatoes

What’s wrong

  • Contains soy, which is not listed on the label.
  • No reactions reported.

Check your kitchen

  • 13.3‑oz vacuum-sealed plastic tray “POWER PLATE MEALS MEATLOAF WITH GARLIC MASHED POTATOES”
  • USE BY dates 6/25/26 through 6/10/27
  • Establishment number “217SEND” inside the USDA mark

Where it was sold

Stores not named in the notice
  • Shipped to distributors in Minnesota, North Dakota and South Dakota.
  • USDA has not posted a store list.
Do this:
  • If you’re allergic to soy, don’t eat it.
  • Throw away or return.
  • Questions: hungry2help@powerplatemeals.com.
