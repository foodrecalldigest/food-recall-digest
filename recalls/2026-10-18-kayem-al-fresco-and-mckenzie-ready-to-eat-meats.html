●  Germs & toxinsUSDA · Oct 17

Possible temperature abuse. Stores and food service nationwide.

Do this: Don't eat them.
Show details

Kayem Foods: ready-to-eat meat and poultry products (hot dogs, bacon, sausage, ham, turkey breast and more)

68 products from 10 brand names are on USDA's list. Check the establishment number inside the USDA mark, then the lot and expiration date.

What’s wrong

  • The meats may have been stored too warm.
  • Kayem Foods kept them in a warehouse cooler that had temperature problems.
  • A retail customer complained on Oct 1 that products were warm when delivered.
  • USDA lists it as High, Class I.
  • The products were made from about March 31 to Oct 7, 2026.
  • About 1,584,361 pounds are recalled.
  • No confirmed reports of illness.

Check your kitchen
