Food Recall Digest

About Food Recall Digest

Food shouldn’t make anyone sick. We check every recall and tell you what to look for in your kitchen.

Why we started

  • New food recalls come out most weeks, and they’re easy to miss.
  • The official notices are long and spread across FDA, USDA and CDC.
  • Checking your kitchen should take a minute, not an afternoon.

What we do every day

  • We read every new food recall and outbreak notice from FDA, USDA and CDC.
  • We pull out what matters: the product, the codes, where it was sold and what to do.
  • We post it here every evening.

How we check the facts

  • Every fact comes from the official notice, and we link to it.
  • We list every product, lot code and UPC the notice names.
  • A store is marked Confirmed only when the notice or a USDA store list names it. Anything else is labeled as a lead.
  • If we get something wrong, we fix it and say so.

What we’re not

  • We’re independent. We’re not a government agency and not affiliated with FDA, USDA or CDC.
  • This isn’t medical advice. If you ate a recalled product and feel sick, call your doctor.

How you can help

  • Share a recall with someone who’d want to know.
  • If it helps you, please consider tipping Pip’s jar on Ko-fi. It keeps the site free.

Get in touch

  • Questions, a tip about a recall or a correction: hello@foodrecalldigest.com
